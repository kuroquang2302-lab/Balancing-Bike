## 2. Power Distribution Architecture

The electrical system uses three isolated voltage rails sharing a single common ground reference (`GND`):

- **12V Motor Drive Rail:**
  - Supplied by a 3S LiPo battery (`11.1 V` nominal, `12.6 V` fully charged).
  - Powers both Nidec 24H BLDC motors directly, with low-ESR electrolytic capacitors decoupling switching transients.
- **5V Logic & Servo Rail:**
  - Regulated from the 12V battery through a `5V / 3A` DC-DC buck converter (e.g., `XL4015`).
  - Powers the TowerPro MG995 steering servo separately from MCU regulators and feeds the `5V` input of the Nucleo-64 and `ESP32-C3 Super Mini`.
- **3.3V Sensor & Encoder Rail:**
  - Generated by the onboard 3.3V regulator on the Nucleo-64.
  - Supplies the MPU-6050 IMU and the `10 kΩ` pull-up resistors required by the open-drain Nidec 24H Hall encoder channels.
